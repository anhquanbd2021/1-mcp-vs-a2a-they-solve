Protocol Layers Lab · two boundaries, two failure modes

Every hop is one of two kinds.

One incident task — why did checkout p95 spike at 14:00? — descends a toy stack: the orchestrator delegates to specialists over A2A-shaped hops, and each specialist reaches its tool over MCP-shaped hops. Inject a failure at either layer and watch the trace tell you exactly where it broke.

Safe educational demo. Protocol shapes, not wire formats — no real MCP server or A2A agent is contacted. Everything runs in the page.
